How it works

Three checks run in a few seconds.

1

Reads the listing

AI vision reads the title, brand, price, seller and the product photo from your screenshot.

2

Runs the checks

It looks for imitation wording, impossible prices, altered brand names and photos that don’t match the text.

3

Gives a risk level

You get a clear level from “Looks genuine” to “Likely fake”, with the reasons behind it.

Spot fakes yourself

Habits that protect you on any shopping app.

“First copy”, “7A”, “master copy”These words openly mean imitation, whatever the photo shows.
Price too good to be trueA branded watch or shoe at a tiny fraction of the usual price is rarely real.
Check the sellerLook for the brand’s official store or an authorised seller badge.
Read the 1–2 star reviewsBuyers often write “not original” or “quality is bad” there.
SafeShop AI is an awareness tool. It estimates risk from a listing and cannot prove that a product is fake or genuine.
