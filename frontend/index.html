Reading the screenshot

This usually takes 10–25 seconds.

How the check works

  1. Reads the screenshot with Google Gemini: brand, price, seller and the listing text.
  2. Looks for copy words like “first copy”, “7A quality” or “replica”, and misspelled brands like “Adibas”.
  3. Compares the price with what the genuine product usually costs.
  4. Checks the seller rating and number of reviews.
  5. Explains the result so you can decide for yourself.
SafeShop AI is a student project. Results are an estimate from the screenshot, not proof. Screenshots are sent to Google Gemini to be read, so don't upload images that show your address, phone number or payment details.
